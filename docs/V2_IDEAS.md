# Version 2: ideas to fold in before the specification is redone

Barry adds ideas here as they occur. Nothing in this file changes the specification
(`docs/Sketchpad-V2-Functional-Specification.pdf`, SPV2-FS-001 Rev A) until the two of us have discussed it.
Then the specification is revised in one pass. Newest last.

| No. | Date | Idea | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| I-1 | 1 Nov 2026 | Plugin engines | Discussed earlier (Sketchpad v2 Architecture, section 10): output engines (SVG, PDF, plotter, DXF, CNC) and input engines (what produces a drawing). Must be settled before CP-0, because it decides how the kernel, generators and renderers are found and loaded. Open question: does "plugin" also cover adding new makers, shapers, connectors and finders by dropping in a file, the same way generators are added? |
| I-2 | 1 Nov 2026 | Every V1 figure opens in V2 | A hard requirement: V2 cannot pass CP-4 until Barry's real figures open and look right. V1's format is fixed and documented in `docs/FIGURE_FORMAT_V1.md` (layer `lid`, `objectVersion`, `role`, `includeInOutput`, named handle discs, `scale`); the import rules are at the end of that file. Each generator needs a "from V1 settings" mapping, written with its feature list. |
| I-3 | 1 Nov 2026 | Parametrics needs its own discussion | The sampling loops far more than usual (Loops x 60 samples per loop: 4,500 points at the default 75, all drawn and searched); reduce it to the usual. Barry's intersection routine works for some curves and is useless for others: find out which and why. Possible addition: curve tracing and simplification (sample more where the curve bends and less where it is straight, and drop points that change nothing), which would also cut the work above. In V1 the infrastructure arm already thins the curve to 720 points (v1-finish item 4). |
| I-4 | 1 Nov 2026 | Simpler commands, built on sets of defaults | Prebuilt sets of components that can be inserted as a group with mostly default values. Isolate the most used kinds of setting (colour, line width) into one standard panel shared by every object, instead of each object declaring them. Standard groupings of settings shown as a tab set. Relates to the settings schema (spec FR-6.4) and the commands table (FR-6.12.2). |
| I-5 | 1 Nov 2026 | A template registry | Generic templates (circles, rectangles, saw tooths, and more) each built by its own controls, kept in a template registry of their own, separate from the object registry, so Barry can add new templates by adding a file. Not covered by the spec so far. V1's guide templates (v1-finish item 6: rectangle, circle, ruler, bounding box, fixed in code) are a first step to replace. |
| I-6 | 1 Nov 2026 | What the legacy adapter must supply | Survey of the 185 pattern and gallery scripts in `docs/V2_LEGACY_SURVEY.md`: 88 paint with a bare `ctx`; 80 use `scriptInfo` controls with a `redrawHandler`; 8 call `armInteractor` themselves; 3 have draggable points; 4 animate; 20 use blending, transparency or gradients and 29 fills or text (these stay playpen scripts with their own canvas); 85 distinct imported names from 29 modules, all listed, plus the `drawState` fields they touch. Voronoi needs `d3-delaunay` copied into `vendor/`. |
| I-7 | 1 Nov 2026 | Intersections (Barry's word for crossings) | Intersections are very changeable: they move, appear and vanish as objects change. Later, perhaps a formulaic way to define them (which curves, which occurrence, by rule) instead of finding them by search. Relates to I-3 (Barry's intersection routine) and to V1's magic points, where a curve's intersections with itself are not offered (v1-finish G-10). |
