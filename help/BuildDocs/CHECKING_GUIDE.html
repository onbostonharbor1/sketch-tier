Checking guide

Barry, 5 November 2026. This is the long form of Home > Checklist. Each section below matches a section of the checklist, and each check has the same id (S1, HM2, P12 ...). For each: exactly what to click, and exactly what you should see. Pictures show what a correct result looks like. Open this page in Home > Help > Build Documents ("Checking guide") beside the checklist, or in VS Code (docs/CHECKING_GUIDE.md).

Words used: the left column is the narrow coloured panel on the left; the subtabs are the second row of tabs (Categories, Errors ...); the caption is the bar under the subtabs with the item's name and a v button at its right, which opens the caption menu; the Commands button is at the top of the left column.

If something does not match: on Home > Checklist type what you saw in the box and press Add item, and tell me.
