Shapers and connectors

Written 5 November 2026. Code: draw/shapers.js, draw/connectors.js, drawPairs in draw/drawUtilities.js. Tests: npm run test:shapers.

Two small libraries of plain functions for the two things that take Sketchpad beyond StringThing: what you can do to an array of points, and ways of stitching.

Shapers: arrays of points in, arrays out

linePoints, arcPoints, reversed, part, everyNth, rotateStart, translated, rotated, scaled, mirrored, resampled, simplified, and for clipping inside, outside, isInside. None changes the array it is given or draws. A moved point is a copy of the old one with x and y changed, so it keeps its class and any z value (depth, for later).

Connectors: arrays in, pairs [a, b] out

pairwise (offset, wrap, and spread for arms of different lengths), corner, chords, growingChords, timesTable, allPairs, polyline, mesh. A connector knows nothing about reversing, ranges or every nth: those are shapers, applied first. Nothing is refused: unequal arms join as far as the shorter goes (or spread across the longer), and odd settings draw what they draw.

Reading a rule

drawPairs(pairwise(part(A, 0, 10), reversed(B)), "blue", 1);

Each step can be looked at on its own in the debugger. drawPairs sends every pair through drawLine, so Final mode, figure transforms, the print aids and stepping all see the lines.

Used by

The curve library and shape palette stitch their points with these connectors (see Curve library and shape palette).

Not yet

The Ideabook routines and the old stitcher still use their own code; moving them onto these is Barry's decision (the promotion rule).
